Cosmology Calculations

About This Project

This is a research-oriented project in the field of Cosmology. Its objective is to develop a comprehensive computational framework for cosmological calculations, ranging from large-scale structure and cosmic expansion simulations to the computation of cosmological parameters and observational quantities. The project aims to bridge theoretical foundations with practical implementations in cosmology. You are warmly invited to collaborate and contribute to the development of this project.
