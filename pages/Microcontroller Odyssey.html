Microcontroller Odyssey

About This Project

🚀 Microcontroller Odyssey – From blinking an LED to talking over UART: a hands-on journey through AVR microcontroller programming in C. This is Part 3 of the Odyssey trilogy (C Odyssey → C++ Odyssey → Microcontroller Odyssey). It is a chapter-by-chapter collection of ATmega32 programs written for CodeVisionAVR, with book examples (EX) and extra problem sets (PS) for every chapter.

📂 Repository Structure :

01 - Digital output & bit operations
02 - Shifts, arrays, 7-segment & simple state machines
03 - Loops & strings
04 - Functions, prototypes, multi-file projects, sprintf/puts
05 - Storage classes & static
06 - Pointers & the LCD library
07 - enum & finite state machines
08 - struct, union & bit-fields
09 - Digital input, debouncing & keypad
10 - External interrupts
11 - Timers/counters, CTC & PWM
12 - Analog-to-digital converter (ADC)
13 - USART serial communication

⚙️ Prerequisites: CodeVisionAVR, Proteus (or real hardware), ATmega32 at 8 MHz, basic knowledge of C.
