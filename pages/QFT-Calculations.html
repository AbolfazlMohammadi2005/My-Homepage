QM/QFT Calculations

About This Project

This is a research-oriented project in the fields of Quantum Mechanics and Quantum Field Theory. Its objective is to develop a comprehensive computational framework for quantum calculations, ranging from scattering amplitudes and Feynman diagram evaluations to perturbative and numerical solutions of quantum systems. The project aims to bridge theoretical foundations with practical implementations in quantum physics. You are warmly invited to collaborate and contribute to the development of this project.
